01 · Audit & Assurance

Independent Audits You Can Stand Behind

An audit is more than a statutory requirement — it is an independent seal of credibility on your financial statements. We plan every audit around your business cycles, test what matters and communicate findings early, so there are no surprises at year-end.

  • Statutory audits under Sri Lanka Accounting & Auditing Standards
  • Internal audits, controls review & risk spot-checks
  • Special purpose investigations & agreed-upon procedures
  • Practical management letters that improve how you operate
02 · Accounting & Bookkeeping

Books That Are Always Current, Never Chaotic

Good decisions need current numbers. We maintain your ledgers, reconciliations and management accounts month by month, so year-end becomes a formality instead of a crisis — and you always know where your business stands.

  • Day-to-day bookkeeping — sales, purchases, cash & bank
  • Monthly management accounts with clear commentary
  • Annual financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, notes)
  • Bank, supplier & customer reconciliations kept clean
03 · Taxation

Compliant, Planned and Filed on Time

Sri Lankan tax rules keep evolving — we keep pace so you do not have to. From corporate income tax to VAT and SSCL, we compute what is due, claim what you are entitled to and file everything with the Inland Revenue Department on schedule.

  • Corporate & personal income tax computations and returns
  • VAT / SSCL registration, monthly returns & reconciliations
  • Withholding tax, WHT certificates & APN support
  • Tax planning advice and IRD query representation
04 · Company Secretarial

Your Company, Registered and In Good Standing

From incorporating your company to keeping its statutory records current, we handle the paperwork that keeps your business legal — so registrar deadlines never become penalties.

  • Private limited company registrations, end to end
  • Annual returns, forms and changes of directors or shares
  • Statutory registers, minutes and secretarial records
  • Guidance on other registrations: PAyE, VAT, EPF/ETF & more
05 · Payroll & EPF / ETF

Salaries Paid Right, Contributions Filed Right

Payroll errors cost goodwill and money. We run accurate payrolls with correct PAYE deductions, prepare payslips, and compute and submit EPF and ETF contributions — every single month.

  • Monthly payroll processing & confidential payslips
  • PAYE (APIT) calculations and employer returns
  • EPF (20/12) & ETF (3/8) schedules — C forms & payments
  • Standing setups for new joiners, leavers and salary changes
06 · Business Advisory

Advice Grounded in Your Actual Numbers

Because we already live inside your accounts, our advice is practical — not generic. We help you plan cash flow, price correctly, prepare for growth and face big decisions with figures, not guesswork.

  • Budgets, cash-flow forecasts & management reviews
  • Feasibility checks for new ventures or expansions
  • Loan documentation & banker-ready financial packs
  • Systems advice — simple processes that keep books clean
